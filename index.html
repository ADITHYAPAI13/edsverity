The Verity Score™

Your skill, measured.
Not self-reported.

Difficulty × Accuracy × Speed

Score = (complexity × accuracy) ÷ (log(time+2) + hints × 0.5). Every submission is calculated, not estimated.

Live and evolving

Scores update with every submission. Inactive? It decays slightly. Consistent? It compounds. Same logic as real-world credibility.

Recruiter-readable percentile

One line tells a hiring manager everything: "Top 8.6%, CSE domain, PAN India." No cover letter required.
